What Makes This Different

Most consultants have been on one side of the table. I have been the bank customer, the implementation lead, and the product builder.

First nCino & TransUnion Integration

I drove the first integration between nCino and TransUnion for consumer credit reporting as the bank customer at ServisFirst Bank. I later designed the same capability at Cync Software across all major credit bureaus, this time from the vendor side.

Three-System Document Automation

I architected an end-to-end loan document automation workflow spanning nCino as the origination layer, a middle-tier server for processing, and a document management platform for intake and sorting. Three distinct systems, one automated pipeline.

Both Sides of the Scale

I implemented nCino at a community bank and trained 250 employees across 20 branches. I now lead a four-platform loan system consolidation at a $200B+ institution. The work is different. The discipline is the same.

AI Solutions for Financial Institutions

Community banks are under pressure to have an AI strategy. Board members are asking questions. Examiners are starting to look for policies. Employees are using ChatGPT on their phones whether anyone approved it or not.

Most banks do not need a custom AI model. They need Microsoft Copilot deployed safely with the right controls, the right policies, and documentation that holds up when the regulators come through.

Assessment

AI Readiness Assessment

A two-week engagement that tells you exactly where you stand. We audit your Microsoft 365 environment, map your data governance gaps, and deliver an examiner-ready report with a concrete next-steps roadmap.

Deployment

Copilot Pilot Program

We deploy Copilot to a controlled group inside your bank. DLP policies, sensitivity labels, conditional access, and audit logging configured. Productivity gains measured. Every control documented for your next examination. Your IT team trained to run it after we leave.

Governance

AI Governance Framework

Board-adoptable policies. Acceptable use documentation. Model and vendor inventory. Incident response procedures. Aligned to FFIEC guidance, OCC supervisory letters, and NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

We have been inside regulated institutions making technology work safely. We are not theorists.
