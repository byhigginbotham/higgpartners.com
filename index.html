What Makes This Different

Most advisors have been on one side of the table. I have been the bank customer, the implementation lead, and the product builder.

First nCino & TransUnion Integration

I drove the first integration between nCino and TransUnion for consumer credit reporting as the bank customer at ServisFirst Bank. I later designed the same capability at Cync Software across all major credit bureaus, this time from the vendor side.

Three-System Document Automation

I architected an end-to-end loan document automation workflow spanning nCino as the origination layer, a middle-tier server for processing, and a document management platform for intake and sorting. Three distinct systems, one automated pipeline.

Operational Implementation

Most consultants hand off and leave. I advise on what happens after go-live: how to build the support team and FTE model to sustain a new system, and how to migrate intentionally from paper-based environments.
